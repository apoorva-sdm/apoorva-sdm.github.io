OVERVIEW

Each year, an estimated 3 crore (30 million) Indians step away from full-time employment to pursue higher education, up-skill, or build their own ventures. While these transitions are often deliberate and growth-oriented, they are rarely recognized because of the lack of external validation or visible markers of progress. It all started with an assumption that Indian adults face a disconnect with their sense of self, self-doubt, a lack of validation and recognition during planned life transitions due to lack of recognition, invisible growth and opportunity, and self doubt.  Float was designed and launched to tackle this exact challenge. Float is a peer-led, co-creative community that makes this period visible. Members gain clarity, value, recognition, validation and reassurance, shaped by the people who are going through it alongside them.

PRIMARY RESEARCH

Research across 20 interviews and four user groups for Float pivoted the service uncovering a core insight.

Insight

Financial discontinuity is a by-product of emotional disruption cause by the loss of a line of reference and anchor points. Float was repositioned to restore emotional discontinuity and thereby financial discontinuity

HYPOTHESIS EXPERIMENTATION

Will a group of Indian adults from different walks of life undergoing similar disconnects with self, be able to co-create a safe space and community for achieving personal growth, confidence and self-esteem?

The bet

Learning from the lived experience of senior batches equips junior batches to navigate uncertainty and ambiguity during academic life transitions.

The read

The digital medium created a disconnect between seniors and juniors, and the expected rapport did not form. Weak ice-breaker and technical glitches added to it.

The call

Move to physical sessions with smaller groups and a more intentional environment.Service form validation.

Extend

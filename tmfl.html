OVERVIEW

TMFL LOS revolutionizes the loan application process, offering seamless application filling alongside robust business rule engine for rapid decision-making automation. Existing processes for both single-vehicle loan journeys and fleet loan journeys, traditionally conducted on paper, were digitized making a significant leap towards efficiency and accessibility for the organization. Design team worked closely with the BPR, Credit and Development teams from Deloitte and the Product team from TMFL to ensure precise UX strategy.

PROBLEM STATEMENT

TMFL’s reliance on pen-and-paper workflows and an Excel-based platform with no UX consideration has caused an abandonment rate of 30% and turnaround times of 20-22 days in the NBFC’s case-processing operations over the past three years, driving applicants away.

CHALLENGE

How might we modernize the NBFC’s case-processing operations, with a streamlined, user-centered digital experience, to shorten turnaround times by 50% while seamlessly integrating single and multi-vehicle LOS flows?

Scheme Module

A rule editor and scheme matrix that let business users create, configure, promote and retire loan schemes themselves, and rules the system cannot check turns into an explicit CPC manual check. Scheme shows complete digitisation for business users: schemes that once needed IT or manual handling can now be run end to end by the business, with the rules checked automatically in the application and CPC.

Scheme: Under Approval

Status tabs with live counts, sortable columns and search help admins reach any scheme among hundreds in second.

Each row shows its approval status, whether it’s fully configured, and an expiry warning (‘Expiring in 5 days!’) ensuring no scheme lapses unnoticed.

Edit, duplicate, comment, promote or create from the same screen, enabling business to runs schemes without waiting on IT.

Scheme: Rule Editor

Admins build eligibility rules in a simple table using the same parameters the application already collects (funding type, residence ownership), rendering an auto check against every scheme on every case.

Rules the system cannot verify becomes an explicit manual check, split into ‘Additional Information to Be Checked’ and ‘Additional Documents to Be Collected’, empowering CPC to verify it exactly.

Rich-text fields let admins write checks in plain language and one Publish action makes the scheme live without a code change.

Central Processing Center Module

A guided, checklist-driven document check with the image beside pre-filled data, closed Yes/No questions, field-level queries and sticky priority for live queries for faster and more consistent verification. CPC was the slowest step in the journey and the biggest lever on disbursal turnaround. Its unique features (clarity question first, field-level queries, keyboard shortcuts, sticky queue) are what made the 2x TAT gain possible.

CPC: Queue List

Cases with a new query or a fresh response are pinned to the top of the queue with sticky priority for live queries to close cases quickly.

Keyboard shortcuts (Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter, Ctrl+E, N, S) let executives move, select, search and check notifications without touching the mouse significantly reducing the overall disbursal TAT by ~24 hours.

CPC: New Case Initiation

The document image with image type and proof type sits beside the pre-filled application data, with zoom, expand controls in a split screen setup, to compare without switching screens or tabs.

Guided Yes/No checklist (1/7), opening with a clarity question, color-coded status notifications, catches unreadable scans early and standardises every check, cutting the cognitive load for CPC users with limited literacy or experience.

CPC: Query Raised Flag

When a check fails, the executive simply answers ‘No’ on that field and the system raises a query for just that item to elicit clarity from the BDM instead of a vague case-level rejection.

Query raised tag at the top and a completed checklist (4/4) keep the executive clear on what has been flagged and that every check is done before the case moves on ensuring high system visibility at all times.

CPC: Raise New Query Pop-up

After checklist completion, a single ‘Raise New Query’ popup lists every field marked ‘No’ ensuring the executive raises all queries for a document in one step instead of raising them one by one.

The popup is raised with built in context and carries the document name, application ID and applicant, with the file one tap away to ensure all necessary information to respond are in one place.

Specific by design Each query is specific by design and is tied to its exact field with a details box, and Submit & Next moves straight to the next document ensuring a precise ask from the BDM and quick pace from the CPC executive.

Custom Query Management Module

A custom master query module that gives every team (CPC, Credit, RCU, Branch Ops) one consistent way to raise a field-level query to the right owner, and gives the BDM/RCU Hunter/CPC Executive one standard way to respond. Queries are how a case moves back and forth between teams when data is flagged deciding much of the turnaround. Design team co-wrote the PRD for this module, which shows ownership beyond screens and into defining how the product works.

LEVERAGING AI AND WHERE IT STOPS

Speculative ERP Features

Where AI Stops

Voice-assisted form filling for BDMs where they speak customer details instead of typing and AI turns the speech into the right form fields

BDM verifies and confirms every highlighted voice-captured field before moving on. Critical fields are read back for confirmation. Typing always stays available as a fallback in noisy dealerships. Customer consent is captured if audio is recorded, with limited model retention.

Users speak or type in their native language and the system stores clean English, with the original kept alongside with AI guided form filling

AI transcribes and the BDM confirms names, ID numbers, amounts and addresses which are shown in both the original and English versions before saving. Proper nouns are transliterated to ensure dedupe and KYC matching don’t break. The original entry is always retained for traceability. Mixed-language speech and low-confidence transcriptions fall back to manual review.

AI suggests the nearest, most available on-field user for KYC and FI cases, ensuring cases reach the right person without manual reassignment

Each suggestion shows why it was chosen and requires TSM approval. Overrides are logged and if the suggestion is unavailable, the existing manual reassignment still works

AI runs the checklist first, comparing each document with pre-filled data and flagging likely mismatches and unclear scans ensuring executives spend their time on exceptions

Each flag shows its confidence, evidence and requires manual approvals. Executives must actively confirm key fields, and the AI answer is occasionally hidden on a few checks to counter automation bias. Passing a document or raising a query require human-in-the-loop interventions.

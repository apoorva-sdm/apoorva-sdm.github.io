Entry Path
How every session is read: three validation signals

A critical path is a chain where failure at one node is carried forward and only becomes visible further down. Seven run through this service. They are not independent: CP2 is the hub, with four other paths feeding into or out of it, and a handful of artefacts sit on several paths at once. Steps marked with a diamond are where the path can break.

The seven paths

Coral means nothing in the blueprint would catch the failure

How the paths connect

Solid lines run forward. Dashed coral lines are the two returns: after a step-back or a nudge, the person re-enters CP2 and the group and addition chain runs again with a different group. Every repeat pass through the service goes through CP2, so a composition rule that holds once has to hold three or four times for the same person.
